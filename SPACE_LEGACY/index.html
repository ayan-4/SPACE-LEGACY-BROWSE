01 / THE IDEA

NOT ABANDONED.
REMEMBERED.

Space exploration leaves more than footprints. Landers, rovers, instruments, and mission hardware remain on other worlds long after their work is done.

SPACE LEGACY is a digital museum for these human-made artifacts — the engineering behind them, the missions they served, and the scientific discoveries they helped make possible.
